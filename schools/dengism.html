01 Origin Vector

Deng Xiaoping survived everything the century aimed at him: France's factories at sixteen, the Long March at thirty, and two complete purges by his own party — 1966, paraded in a dunce cap, his son crippled by Red Guards; 1976, purged again months before Mao's death. He returned from each exile smaller, quieter and holding more of the apparatus. By December 1978, at the Third Plenum, the twice-buried pragmatist held the deck: "practice is the sole criterion of truth" replaced the "two whatevers" of Mao's designated heirs, and Reform and Opening (改革开放) became the line.

The founding settlement was judicial and theological at once. The Gang of Four went on televised trial; the 1981 Resolution on Party History delivered the verdict on the founder himself — Mao was "70% correct, 30% wrong," his Cultural Revolution a "catastrophe," his portrait staying on the gate. The formula let the party condemn its past without condemning itself: continuity of chassis, total replacement of engine. Orthodox Maoists at home and abroad called it capitalist restoration with extra steps — Schism №13 — and the archive notes their case has only accumulated exhibits since.

02 Core Doctrine

The primary stage of socialism

The load-bearing thesis: China is in socialism's primary stage, which may last a century or more, and the task of that stage is developing the productive forces — by whatever works, markets included. Orthodoxy's timetable replaced with a geological one; every liberalization pre-authorized by definition.

The cat theory

"It doesn't matter whether the cat is black or white, so long as it catches mice" — Deng's 1962 heresy, promoted to state philosophy. Results over doctrine; growth as the measure of truth. The archive's shortest complete ideology.

The socialist market economy

Special Economic Zones as laboratories (Shenzhen: fishing village to megacity in one generation), household-responsibility plots replacing communes, price signals replacing quotas — under a party that owns the commanding heights and every general's commission. Market as tool, Leninism as owner.

Four Cardinal Principles

The non-negotiables fencing the experiment: the socialist road, the people's democratic dictatorship, the party's leadership, Marxism–Leninism–Mao Zedong Thought. Everything is reformable except the reformer's monopoly — the clause Tiananmen enforced.

Hide and bide

Foreign policy as patience: "hide your strength, bide your time, never claim leadership." Retired from service circa 2013 by the current management, which the archive files as this doctrine's one sunset clause.

04 In Practice

The results ledger is without precedent in economic history: roughly 800 million people lifted from absolute poverty over four decades — the largest and fastest such movement ever recorded — GDP doubling and redoubling from a base smaller than the Netherlands' to the world's second (by some measures first) economy, and the archive's only file currently operating aircraft carriers. The debit column is equally concrete: Tiananmen, 1989 — the Fourth Cardinal Principle enforced by the PLA against students beneath Mao's portrait, casualty count still a state secret — plus inequality at Gilded Age levels in a nominally proletarian state, systemic corruption the current management's signature campaign both fights and wields, and a surveillance apparatus every other file in this archive would have called science fiction and requisitioned immediately.

Under Xi Jinping the pendulum has swung back toward the party: term limits abolished (2018), "Xi Jinping Thought" in the constitution, tech billionaires re-educated in humility, and Marxism — genuinely, textually — back on the curriculum. Whether this is Dengism's correction, completion or quiet repeal is the current decade's live question; the archive keeps the file open and the pencil sharpened.

05 Schisms & Enemies

  • vs. the Gang of Four (1976–1981). The founding purge-and-trial — Mao's widow Jiang Qing defiant in the dock ("I was Chairman Mao's dog; whomever he told me to bite, I bit"). Sentence: death, commuted; verdict on the era: catastrophe, official.
  • vs. world Maoism. For Sendero, the Naxalites and the orthodox parties, Deng is the arch-revisionist — the "capitalist roader" Mao's posters warned about, vindicated by every stock exchange in Shanghai. Schism №13; the people's wars fight on under the banner of a China that repudiated them.
  • vs. the liberal reading (1989). The other direction's schism: those who thought economic opening implied political opening met the Four Cardinal Principles in Tiananmen Square. Zhao Ziyang, the party's own general secretary, took the students' side and spent his remaining sixteen years under house arrest — the file's internal casualty.
  • vs. every other file in the building, definitionally. Hoxhaists, Trotskyists, left communists and anarchists agree on almost nothing except that FILE 20 is not communism; FILE 20's answer — the primary stage, the 800 million, the century-long clock — does not require their agreement and has not requested it.

07 Legacy

Dengism closes the archive because it reopened the whole argument: it is either communism's greatest adaptation — Leninism completing, at last, the development Marx said socialism required — or its most successful abandonment, a red flag flown over the world's most dynamic capitalism. What is not arguable: the largest poverty reduction in human history, the survival of the party-state form every other file lost, and the fact that the twenty-first century's defining geopolitical contest is between liberal capitalism and this file — an outcome not one other dossier in this building predicted. The tree's last branch is, for now, its tallest. The archive does not forecast. It files.
