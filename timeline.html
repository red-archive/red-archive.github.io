MASTER CHRONOLOGY // 1516 – PRESENT // ALL SCHOOLS

THE TIMELINE

Five centuries of the communist idea, from a lawyer's Latin daydream to a nuclear superpower's five-year plans and back down to meme pages. Tags mark which school each event spawned, split or buried.

01 // THE DREAMERS — 1516–1847

  • 1516UTOPIAN
    Thomas More publishes Utopia

    A satirical island without private property gives the whole genre its name — and its accusation: "no-place."

  • 1796UTOPIAN
    Babeuf's Conspiracy of the Equals

    Gracchus Babeuf plots to complete the French Revolution with common ownership. Guillotined — but the idea of revolutionary communism is born.

  • 1808–1825UTOPIAN
    Fourier's phalansteries, Owen's New Harmony, Saint-Simon's industrial gospel

    The three great system-builders design cooperative societies on paper and in Indiana. Most collapse within years.

  • 1840ANARCHIST
    Proudhon: "Property is theft"

    The first self-declared anarchist opens the other great anti-capitalist lineage — the one that will refuse the state itself.

  • 1844MARXISM
    Marx meets Engels in Paris

    A ten-day conversation in the Café de la Régence begins the most consequential writing partnership in political history.

02 // THE FOUNDATION — 1848–1888

  • 1848MARXISM
    The Communist Manifesto; revolutions sweep Europe

    "A spectre is haunting Europe." The revolutions fail, but the program — abolition of private property in the means of production — is now printed.

  • 1864INTERNATIONAL
    First International founded in London

    Marxists, anarchists, mutualists and trade unionists in one room. It lasts eight years before the room explodes.

  • 1867MARXISM
    Capital, Volume I

    Surplus value: the claim that profit is unpaid labour time, dressed in 800 pages of political economy.

  • 1871COMMUNE
    The Paris Commune

    Seventy-two days of workers' government, then 20,000 dead in Bloody Week. Every school will claim its lesson — and each draws a different one.

  • 1872SCHISM №1
    The Hague Congress: Marx expels Bakunin

    State or no state — the first great split. The anarchist lineage separates permanently from the Marxist one.

  • 1876–1880ANARCHIST
    Anarcho-communism crystallizes

    Cafiero, Malatesta and Kropotkin fuse anarchism with communist distribution: from each according to ability, to each according to need — without a state to enforce it.

  • 1883MARXISM
    Marx dies in London

    Eleven mourners attend. Engels promises the graveside that the name will "endure through the ages." The fight over the inheritance begins immediately.

03 // THE INTERNATIONALS — 1889–1917

  • 1889ORTHODOX
    Second International founded

    Mass socialist parties across Europe, led by the German SPD — the "orthodox" church of Marxism, with Kautsky as its pope.

  • 1895DE LEONISM
    De Leon's Socialist Trade & Labor Alliance

    In America, Daniel De Leon fuses ballot-box socialism with revolutionary industrial unionism — a blueprint Lenin later praises.

  • 1899SCHISM №2
    Bernstein's Evolutionary Socialism

    "The movement is everything, the final goal nothing." The revisionism debate splits reform from revolution inside every party in Europe.

  • 1902LENINISM
    Lenin: What Is To Be Done?

    Workers left alone reach only trade-union consciousness; revolution requires a vanguard of professionals. The century's most consequential pamphlet.

  • 1903SCHISM №3
    Bolshevik–Menshevik split

    The Russian party splits over one clause in the membership rules. The hard kernel — Bolsheviks, "majority-ites" — is Lenin's.

  • 1905LUXEMBURGISM
    Russian Revolution of 1905; the mass strike

    Petersburg workers invent the soviet. Rosa Luxemburg reads the wave of strikes as the true engine of revolution — spontaneity over command.

  • 1914SCHISM №4
    The Second International votes for war

    Socialist parties back their own governments' war credits. Internationalism collapses in an afternoon; Lenin and Luxemburg never forgive it.

  • 1917LENINISM
    February, then October

    The Tsar falls; eight months later the Bolsheviks storm the Winter Palace. For the first time, communists hold a state.

04 // THE SPLIT DECADES — 1918–1945

  • 1918–19LUXEMBURGISM
    German Revolution; Luxemburg and Liebknecht murdered

    The Spartacist rising is crushed by Freikorps under a Social-Democratic government. Luxemburg's body is thrown in the Landwehr Canal; her school becomes a martyrology.

  • 1919COMINTERN
    Third International (Comintern) founded in Moscow

    Twenty-one conditions bind every member party to the Bolshevik model. World communism now has a headquarters — and a chain of command.

  • 1920SCHISM №6
    Lenin: "Left-Wing" Communism: An Infantile Disorder

    The council communists of Germany and Holland, and Bordiga's abstentionists in Italy, are read out of the church by the man they helped win.

  • 1921LEFT COM
    Kronstadt rebellion crushed; NEP begins

    The sailors of 1917 rise against party dictatorship under the slogan "soviets without Bolsheviks" — and are machine-gunned across the ice. For the communist left, the revolution's death certificate.

  • 1923WESTERN
    Lukács' History and Class Consciousness; Frankfurt School founded

    If the revolution failed in the West, the reasons must be cultural. Marxism turns inward: ideology, reification, hegemony.

  • 1924ML
    Lenin dies; Stalin coins "Leninism"

    Stalin's Foundations of Leninism freezes the doctrine into a catechism — Marxism–Leninism — with himself as its interpreter.

  • 1927–29SCHISM №7
    Trotsky expelled, then exiled

    Permanent revolution loses to socialism in one country. The Left Opposition is broken; Trotsky begins thirteen years of exile with a pen.

  • 1935MAOISM
    Zunyi Conference: Mao takes the Chinese party

    Mid-Long March, the CCP turns from Moscow's urban blueprint to Mao's: the peasantry as revolutionary class, the countryside as base.

  • 1936–38ML
    The Great Terror and the Moscow Trials

    The Old Bolsheviks confess to absurdities and are shot; roughly 700,000 are executed. Every rival school dates its indictment of Stalinism from here.

  • 1936–39ANARCHIST
    Spanish Revolution and Civil War

    Anarchist Catalonia collectivizes; in May 1937 Stalinists suppress the POUM and CNT in Barcelona's streets. The left's civil war inside the civil war.

  • 1938TROTSKYISM
    Fourth International founded

    Trotsky's answer to the Comintern: a world party against both capitalism and Stalinism. It splinters almost annually thereafter.

  • 1940TROTSKYISM
    Trotsky assassinated in Coyoacán

    An NKVD agent's ice axe ends the co-leader of October. The school survives its founder as a diaspora of quarreling internationals.

05 // COLD WAR HERESIES — 1946–1970

  • 1948SCHISM №8
    Tito says no: Yugoslavia expelled from the Cominform

    The first ruling party to defy Stalin survives it — and invents self-management socialism and non-alignment to justify the divorce.

  • 1949MAOISM
    People's Republic of China proclaimed

    "The Chinese people have stood up." A quarter of humanity is now governed by a communist party — one that took power its own way.

  • 1953ML
    Stalin dies

    The succession struggle ends with Khrushchev — and with a question no one had dared ask aloud: what, exactly, was all that?

  • 1955JUCHE
    Kim Il-sung's "Juche" speech

    Against Soviet and Chinese tutelage alike, the DPRK proclaims self-reliance — the seed of a state ideology that will outgrow Marx entirely.

  • 1956SCHISM №9
    The Secret Speech; Hungary invaded

    Khrushchev denounces Stalin's cult and crimes; months later Soviet tanks crush the Hungarian workers' councils. Millions leave Western parties. Every later split starts here.

  • 1958–62MAOISM
    The Great Leap Forward

    Backyard furnaces and communes were meant to overtake Britain; instead famine kills tens of millions. Mao retreats — temporarily.

  • 1959GUEVARISM
    Cuban Revolution

    Castro and Guevara take Havana with a guerrilla column, not a vanguard party. A new template: the foco.

  • 1960–63SCHISM №10
    The Sino-Soviet split goes public

    Peaceful coexistence versus people's war; "revisionists" versus "adventurists." The world movement tears into two rival Romes.

  • 1961HOXHAISM
    Albania breaks with Moscow

    Enver Hoxha sides with Beijing and keeps Stalin's statues up. Tirana becomes the Vatican of anti-revisionism.

  • 1962POSADISM
    Posadas founds his own Fourth International

    The Argentine Trotskyist's current becomes legend: welcome nuclear war, talk to dolphins, and assume any civilization capable of interstellar travel is communist.

  • 1961–69AUTONOMISM
    Quaderni Rossi and Italy's Hot Autumn

    Operaismo inverts the telescope: study workers' refusal, not capital's plans. Wildcat strikes make the factory ungovernable.

  • 1966–76MAOISM
    The Cultural Revolution

    "Bombard the headquarters": Mao unleashes the Red Guards on his own party. A decade of upheaval, persecution and factional civil war.

  • 1967GUEVARISM
    Che executed in Bolivia

    The foco fails its own test; its commander becomes the century's most reproduced image.

  • 1968WESTERN
    Paris May; Prague Spring crushed

    Students quote Marcuse and the Situationists; Warsaw Pact tanks answer Dubček. New Left and old orthodoxy diverge for good.

06 // LATE SIGNALS — 1971–NOW

  • 1972JUCHE
    Juche enters the DPRK constitution

    Marxism–Leninism is progressively edited out of North Korea's founding documents; the Leader's thought replaces it.

  • 1973–77EUROCOM
    Eurocommunism: Berlinguer, Carrillo, Marchais

    After Chile's coup, the Italian, Spanish and French parties embrace pluralism, elections and — scandalously — NATO's shelter. Moscow fumes.

  • 1976MAOISM
    Mao dies; the Gang of Four falls

    Within a month of the funeral, the Cultural Revolution's stewards are under arrest. The verdict on the era is being drafted.

  • 1978SCHISM №11/13
    Deng's reform era opens; China–Albania split

    "Practice is the sole criterion of truth." Markets return under party control; Hoxha declares China social-imperialist and bunkers down alone.

  • 1980MAOISM
    Sendero Luminoso begins its people's war in Peru

    Gonzalo's "Marxism–Leninism–Maoism" systematizes Maoism as a universal third stage — exported later to Nepal and India.

  • 1989–91ML
    Tiananmen; the Wall falls; the USSR dissolves

    The flag comes down over the Kremlin on Christmas Day 1991. Of the great party-states, only China, Vietnam, Laos, Cuba and the DPRK remain.

  • 1994AUTONOMISM
    Zapatista uprising in Chiapas

    An indigenous army that refuses to seize the state; autonomist and anarchist ideas return in a ski mask.

  • 1996–2008MAOISM
    Nepal's People's War; Naxalite insurgency peaks

    Maoism remains the only school still waging live guerrilla wars — even as Nepal's Maoists trade rifles for ministries.

  • 2008–NOWDENGISM
    Crisis, revival, memes

    The financial crash sends Capital back up the sales charts; the CPC becomes the largest ruling party on earth; the arguments in this archive continue nightly, online, under worse usernames.
