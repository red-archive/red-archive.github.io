RED ARCHIVE

Twenty schools. Five centuries. One long argument about who owns the future. This archive maps every major current of communist thought — where each one spawned, what it believed, whom it fought and what it became — as a living tree of descent, influence and schism. A history file with a line of its own, argued rather than assumed: the purges, famines and heresies are documented alongside the theory.
